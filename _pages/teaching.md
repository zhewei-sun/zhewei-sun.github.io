- Co-Instructor: Toyota Technological Institute (TTI-Japan)
  * Machine Learning II - Spring 2026
  * This course serves as an introduction to deep learning. Topics include fundamental building blocks of modern deep neural networks, backpropagation, representation learning, convolutional and recurrent networks, deep reinforcement learning, and their applications in computer vision, natural language processing, and robotics. By the end of the course, students will grasp both foundational concepts of deep learning and how such concepts can be applied in real-world AI systems. This course is intended for upper-year undergraduates and graduate students in computer science or related disciplines.
